An original top-down 2D open-world crime game in plain Canvas — no vendored
library, zero network requests. Drive, steal cars, outrun the police, and run
three story missions for fixer Lena Torres across a 3400×2600 city with
roads, parks, a beach and sea, and landmarks: garage, hotel, hospital,
police HQ, city hall, marina, docks, airfield and safehouse.

## What's in the game

- 22 traffic cars plus parked cars; 46 pedestrians who flee gunfire
- Car theft (E) with a wanted level of 0–5 stars; police cars and foot
  officers who chase, ram and bust — wanted decays when unseen
- 3 timed story missions: First Fare, The Marina Pickup, The Great Getaway
- Taxi fares, street cash and health packs; on-foot pistol combat against
  rival thugs
- HUD (cash, health, wanted stars, district, stats) plus a live minimap;
  title / pause / help / wasted / busted screens; localStorage save
- Synthesised WebAudio (engine, sirens, gunshots, jingles — no audio files)

## Controls

WASD / arrows to move and drive, Shift to run, E to enter or steal a car and
to start missions, Space handbrake, mouse aim + click to shoot, H horn,
R restart mission, P pause, M mute.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open the served page in a browser. The game also runs by opening
`game/index.html` directly.
