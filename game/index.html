SUNSET BAY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY

Rico Vale is out, broke and back in Sunset Bay. Mara Quinn has three jobs — and the whole city is for the taking.

JOB 1 Borrowed Wheels — steal a car, bring it to Mara's Garage.

JOB 2 The Package — docks to the Palms Hotel in 90 seconds.

JOB 3 The Big Score — rob Bay Federal Bank and escape to the safehouse.

WASD move / drive  ·  Mouse aim  ·  Click shoot

E enter / exit car  ·  R reload  ·  Shift sprint  ·  Space handbrake

P pause  ·  M mute  ·  Progress autosaves

A completely original game. Sunset Bay, Rico Vale, Mara Quinn and every street, car and story beat are invented here — no borrowed names, art or music. Runs fully offline.
